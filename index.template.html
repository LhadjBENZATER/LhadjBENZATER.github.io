ABOUT ME

Research across the
signal chain.

iD0009-0006-2533-0315

I am a telecommunications engineer and PhD candidate at École Militaire Polytechnique (EMP), Algiers, working on cooperative compressed spectrum sensing for cognitive radio networks.

My research brings together theoretical modeling, sparse recovery algorithms and experimental validation. I study how cooperating receivers can make reliable spectrum decisions from compressed measurements, and how acquisition architectures and RF front ends affect those decisions.

Using MATLAB, Python, GNU Radio and USRP platforms, I connect algorithm development with the constraints of practical sensing systems.
